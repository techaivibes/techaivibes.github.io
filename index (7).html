A Simple Weekly Content Plan for Faceless Creators

A repeatable content planning system for faceless creators making useful short-form videos with a phone.

Choose a few repeatable content pillars and batch research, scripting, recording and review to keep production manageable.

Pick three content pillars

For an AI-focused page, useful pillars might be quick tool demos, prompt tutorials and honest workflow breakdowns. A small set of pillars makes it easier for viewers to understand what the account offers and helps you plan ideas consistently.

Give each day a job

For example: Monday, collect questions; Tuesday, verify a tool or tip; Wednesday, draft scripts; Thursday, record screen demos; Friday, edit and caption; Saturday, publish or test a new format; Sunday, review performance and collect next week’s ideas. Adjust this to your actual schedule.

Use a repeatable video template

A simple template is hook, demonstration, result and next step. Keep one idea per video. For faceless visuals, record a relevant workflow, use original slides, add captions and hide private information. Make sure you have permission to use any third-party material.

Keep an idea bank

Record the audience question, proposed hook, source to verify, required screen recording and status. This prevents repeated brainstorming and helps you notice which topics need better evidence.

Review signals, not just views

Look at watch time, completion, saves, shares and meaningful comments where available. Compare similar formats rather than drawing conclusions from a single post. Use the results to test one change at a time.

Keep the workload sustainable

A plan should fit your available time. Reuse a consistent editing style, but do not publish inaccurate or repetitive content just to meet a streak. Quality and usefulness matter more than an arbitrary volume target.

Key takeaway

A content calendar reduces friction. Keep the process light enough to repeat, and use audience feedback to improve the next batch.
